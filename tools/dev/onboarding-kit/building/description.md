Our building has three floors. You come in from the street into the lobby, with the café open to
it on the right. A door at the back left leads to the stairs, and the one in the middle to the
studio, which runs along the back of the building with a big window onto the garden.

Upstairs there are two flats at the front, with a landing and a little library behind them. A
second staircase at the back right goes up to the top floor: a long loft across the front and a
landing at the back, with a door out onto a small roof terrace. A lift goes from the studio to the
library and up to the top landing.

The plans are one per floor, in order: plan-1 is the ground floor, plan-2 the first floor and
plan-3 the top floor. Floors are oak upstairs and polished concrete on the ground floor.
