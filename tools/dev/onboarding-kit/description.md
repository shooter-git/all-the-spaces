Our flat is on the third floor. The front door opens into a long hallway that runs the whole
depth of the flat. On the left are the two bedrooms with the bathroom between them; on the right,
the study near the front door, and at the far end the living room with the kitchen along its
back wall. The balcony is off the living room. Floors are oak, the bathroom is white tiles, and
the walls are a warm off-white. The study has a desk under the window and a big bookshelf.
