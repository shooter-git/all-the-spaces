# Brand

The mark is three sheets of washi, one behind another, each a little further along: spaces behind
spaces. Back to front they're the three spaces the app is about: reality (warm sand, `#DDB08F`),
the place we share (sage, `#94BBA0`) and what we create in it (indigo, `#4A5FA6`). They're
translucent, like paper held up to the light, and drawn by hand (soft, uneven edges), soft
pastels with just enough colour to tell them apart. The interface is washi paper and sumi ink with
ai-iro (Japanese indigo, `#3F5B7C`) as the single strong colour and muted natural tones (sky,
stone, moss, unbleached silk).

- `node tools/brand-mark.mjs` draws the mark (from fixed seeds, so it's the same every time) and
  writes the masters in `brand/` (`mark.svg`, `icon.svg`, `favicon.svg`, `mark-mono.svg`) and the
  interface's inline copy (`packages/ui/src/mark-paths.ts`). `logo.svg` and `wordmark.svg` are
  generated by `pnpm brand:build`.
- `pnpm brand:build` regenerates every icon, favicon, splash screen and social image.
- Interface colours and type are design tokens in `packages/ui/src/tokens.css`.
- The name and mark never include the names or marks of AI vendors.
- Forks that are run as a different product should use their own name and mark.

## Sayings on the door

The card that opens before you go into a creation shows one saying at random, on the right of the
mark: in its own language and script, in a hand that suits it, with exactly what it means and who
said it. There are 35, from Rumi and Laozi to Dante, Dickinson and a Hawaiian welcome
(`tools/door-phrases.mjs`).

- Add only lines whose original wording and meaning are settled, with their source. Where a famous
  line exists in more than one version, say which one is used.
- No paraphrases (most "Rumi quotes" in English are modern rewrites), and nothing still in
  copyright.
- `node tools/door-phrases.mjs` draws them into `apps/web/public/doors/` (shaped with HarfBuzz, so
  every script joins and stacks properly; the fonts themselves aren't shipped).
